# Keep Vigie running in the background

Vigie can keep watch with its window closed, scan every site on its own and tell you when
something turns urgent. Everything is off until you turn it on, in **Settings, General, In the
background**.

## Keep Vigie running

Closing the window leaves Vigie in the menu bar. Its eye opens Vigie, scans every site, shows when
the next automatic scan is due and how many sites are urgent. ⌘Q always quits.

Then, if you want, **Open at login**: Vigie starts with your session, window closed.

## Automatic scans

Every 1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours, Vigie scans every site, as **Scan all sites** does. It never updates
anything. The foot of the site list shows when the next one is due. A computer that slept runs one
scan when it wakes.

## Notifications

Vigie tells you when a scan turns a site urgent, or cannot reach a host any more. Never for what
was already known, never for what got better. Everything is also kept in **Activity**.

Learn more: [Vigie in the background, and scans with Vigie closed](../background.md).
